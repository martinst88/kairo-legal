Privacy Policy

Búzios · Tarot · Lenormand

2026-10-29 · Android 1.7 · org.kairoworks.buzios

1. Publisher and scope

Kairo Works, the development brand of Thiago Rosa Martins, publishes org.kairoworks.buzios. This policy describes Cowries, Tarot and Lenormand in version 1.7. Earlier versions may use interstitial ads and one-hour benefits. Contact: privacy@kairoworks.org.

2. Local readings and journal

Questions, notes, cards, cowrie results and preferences stay on the device, in SQLite, app preferences or browser storage. We have no reading server or mandatory login. We do not send this content, Odù, reading themes or religious subjects to AdMob or Analytics. The app does not request GPS location or birth dates. Android version 1.7 disables automatic app backup.

3. Ads and consent

Each oracle has one free daily reading. Extra readings may be unlocked through a rewarded ad chosen by the user or a subscription. Google AdMob may process IP address and approximate region, device and advertising identifiers, interactions and diagnostics for ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention. We request non-personalized ads; this does not eliminate all SDK collection. Google’s platform checks consent, which can be reviewed in Settings when available.

4. Optional metrics

Google Analytics for Firebase is off by default. If enabled in Settings, the SDK may process an installation identifier, sessions, technical information, approximate region and generic reading, ad and purchase events. We send no reading parameters, oracle, questions, cards, notes or religion. Analytics ad personalization and its advertising ID collection are disabled. You can withdraw consent; new metrics stop and the local identifier is reset. This does not automatically delete previously sent events.

5. Purchases

Subscriptions and payments are processed by Google Play. The app checks products, prices and purchase status to unlock and restore access. Kairo Works does not receive full payment-card details. Google retains transaction records under its policies and obligations. Purchase data may also appear in Firebase metrics when that collection is enabled.

6. Sharing, reminders and contact

Image sharing opens the system share menu; questions and notes are excluded from the image. You choose the destination. Reminders are local and optional. Directory applications and support requests open voluntary email contact; Kairo Works and its email providers receive the message and chosen attachments. Professional profiles are published only with documented permission and review.

7. Retention, deletion and security

The journal remains until you delete entries, clear data or uninstall the app. Credits are local and do not transfer between devices. Google service data follows those providers’ retention periods and controls. Support messages are kept as needed for assistance, security and applicable obligations. We do not sell your reading content. Communications with Google use TLS. Infrastructure services may log IP and technical information when this policy is opened; providers may process data outside your country. No protection eliminates every risk of loss or unauthorized access.

8. Rights, audience and changes

For access, correction, deletion or information requests, email privacy@kairoworks.org. For device-only data, we will explain local deletion. We may need information to locate records in external services. The app is not specifically directed at children; follow its rating and device supervision settings. Policy changes are indicated by the update date. Support: support@kairoworks.org.
